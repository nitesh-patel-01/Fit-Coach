Free AI Fitness App for India

Fit Coach is a free AI-powered personal trainer built for Indian users. Get personalised gym workouts, home workouts without equipment, Indian vegetarian and non-vegetarian diet plans, BMI calculator, voice coach, water tracker, weight tracker, body measurements, daily streak, and achievement gamification — all on one page, all private.

Features

  • Personalised weekly workout schedule (3 to 6 days per week)
  • Push / Pull / Legs, Full Body, and Bro Split training plans
  • Gender-aware exercises with proper rep and rest timing
  • Indian diet with BMR, TDEE and macro targets (protein, carbs, fats)
  • Voice trainer with Indian English accent
  • Full timer mode with countdowns and set tracking
  • Weight, measurement, water and streak tracking
  • XP, levels and achievements to keep you motivated
  • Fully offline after first load, zero data sent to any server

Who is Fit Coach for?

Beginners starting their fitness journey, intermediate lifters who want structured programming, and advanced athletes tracking progress. Works for men, women, gym-goers, and home-workout users across India.
